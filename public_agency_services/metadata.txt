about=RestWrangler wrangles public ArcGIS REST services from ArcGIS Online and ArcGIS Enterprise into your current QGIS project. Browse service directories, identify layers by their item or service names, see layer and service counts, and select individual layers or entire groups with one click. Organize connections into custom nested groups or preserve the service hierarchy. Supports FeatureServer layers and tables, plus MapServer layers as server-styled map images or queryable vectors where supported. Save and share a standard QGIS project so colleagues can use essential public agency data without an Esri license or this plugin installed. An internet connection and accessible public services are required; data is not downloaded for offline use.
